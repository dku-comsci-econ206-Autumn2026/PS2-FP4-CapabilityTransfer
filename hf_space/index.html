What this is

Nearly all frontier capability now moves by distillation. After distilling, a firm picks one of the four cells above. Paying for disclosure is the obvious policy, but a flat prize pays the free-rider as much as the originator, so nobody builds the original.

This page locates two thresholds on k. Below both, the market is all secrecy. Between them, everyone discloses but nobody innovates: all imitation. Above both, the two coexist. Paying per unit of E is what makes the second threshold exist at all.
