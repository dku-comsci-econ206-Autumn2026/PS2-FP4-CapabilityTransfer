Nearly all frontier capability now moves by distillation. After distilling, a firm lands in one of the four cells. Paying for disclosure is the obvious policy — but how you pay decides whether anyone still builds the original.

Flat pays free-ride and originate the same amount, so originate is always short by ΔC and never wins: the market ends at all-imitation. Per-unit pays the originator for the larger budget it ran, which is the only reason a second threshold exists — above it, originate and disclose coexist.
