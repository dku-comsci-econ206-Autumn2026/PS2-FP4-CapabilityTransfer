Prize Rate Game

Pay for training, not for openness. Four decisions: a firm distils or trains, then keeps the model closed for monopoly profit π or opens it for a reward kE while losing exclusivity L.

Nearly all frontier capability now moves by distillation, and distillation is cheap but never free. Current rules reward openness, so a distilled copy and an original source earn the same — which is why the market can empty of sources.

Indexing the reward by training compute E separates the two open cells: the originator collects the largest prize and the imitator the smallest. Paid flat, or capped, the two cells are paid alike and nobody has a reason to train.
