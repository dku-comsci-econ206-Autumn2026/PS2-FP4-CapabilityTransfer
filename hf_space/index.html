Prize Rate Game

Pay for training, not for openness. Four decisions: a firm distils or trains, then keeps the model closed for monopoly profit π or opens it for a reward kE while losing exclusivity L. An open release also buys a share of the next generation of infrastructure, worth βφK·m/(m+m0).

The private ranking (1) > (3) > (2) > (4) exactly reverses the social ranking (4) > (2) > (3) > (1) — while the future-share motive is weak. With a strong motive, opening can become privately attractive, which is why an unregulated market can fill with open copies rather than stay closed.

Panel (a) switches the future-share motive off; panel (b) switches it on. With the motive off, no reward leaves the market closed (state 1). Nearly all frontier capability now moves by distillation, and distillation is cheap but never free. Current rules reward openness, so a distilled copy and an original source earn the same — which is why the market can empty of sources even when firms open voluntarily.

Indexing the reward by training compute E separates the two open cells: the originator collects the largest prize and the imitator the smallest. Paid flat, or capped, the two cells are paid alike and nobody has a reason to train.
